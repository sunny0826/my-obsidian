## Main Topic
A curated collection of 55 fundamental laws, principles, and mental models that govern software systems, engineering teams, and technical decision-making — compiled from computer science, management theory, and systems thinking.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. 软件工程中最重要的 7 大维度（架构、团队、规划、质量、规模、设计、决策）
2. 各维度最具代表性的定律及其核心含义
3. 这些定律如何相互关联，帮助在实际工作中做出更好的技术决策

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Level**: Intermediate to Expert（具备一定编程经验的工程师）
- **Context**: 自我学习、知识整理、技术视野拓展
- **Expectations**: 快速了解软件工程的核心理论框架

## Content Type Analysis
- **Data Structure**: 按类别（Category）分组的信息集合，每组包含多条定律
- **Key Relationships**: 55 条定律分布于 7 个类别，同一名称的定律可能跨类别出现
- **Visual Opportunities**: 每类用一个模块展示，模块内列出定律名+一句话描述

## Key Data Points (Verbatim)
- "Premature optimization is the root of all evil." — Knuth
- "Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later." — Brooks's Law
- "Given enough eyeballs, all bugs are shallow." — Linus's Law
- "Debugging is twice as hard as writing the code in the first place." — Kernighan's Law
- "Work expands to fill the time available for its completion." — Parkinson's Law
- "Every application has an inherent amount of irreducible complexity." — Tesler's Law
- "Organizations design systems that mirror their own communication structure." — Conway's Law
- "A distributed system can guarantee only two of: consistency, availability, and partition tolerance." — CAP Theorem
- "The value of a network is proportional to the square of the number of users." — Metcalfe's Law
- "80% of the problems result from 20% of the causes." — Pareto Principle
- "Anything that can go wrong will go wrong." — Murphy's Law
- "We tend to overestimate the effect of a technology in the short run and underestimate the impact in the long run." — Amara's Law
- "The square root of the total number of participants does 50% of the work." — Price's Law
- "The first 90% of the code accounts for the first 90% of development time; the remaining 10% accounts for the other 90%." — Ninety-Ninety Rule
- "Every piece of knowledge must have a single, unambiguous, authoritative representation." — DRY Principle
- "A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that worked." — Gall's Law
- "Don't add functionality until it is necessary." — YAGNI
- "Be conservative in what you do, be liberal in what you accept from others." — Postel's Law
